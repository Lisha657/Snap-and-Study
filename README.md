📚 Snap & Study

Snap & Study is an AI-powered learning assistant that helps students understand difficult questions, diagrams, textbook pages, and study notes. Students can upload images or PDFs and receive clear, step-by-step explanations powered by Google Gemini AI.

Features

Upload photos of questions, diagrams, and handwritten notes.

Upload PDF study materials for explanation.

Ask questions and interact with an AI tutor.

Get simple, structured explanations of complex concepts.

Email study summaries for later revision.

Technologies Used

Python

Streamlit

Google Gemini AI

Gmail SMTP

Git and GitHub

How It Works

Enter your name and email address.

Upload a study image or PDF, or type a question.

Gemini analyzes the material and generates an explanation.

Review the explanation in the chat.

Click Send to Email to receive a study summary.
